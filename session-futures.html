🔬 Engine A cross-check (independent data)

To check whether these results are real or just artifacts of one data feed, I re-ran the exact same strategy in a second, completely independent engine — my own Python backtester on yfinance futures data (vs TradingView for Engine C). Compared on the 2-year window (yfinance's 1-hour history limit), best of 2R/3R:

The engines agree only 53% of the time on whether a contract is even profitable (17 of 32) — close to a coin-flip. That is the headline finding: at the individual-contract level these results are not robust — they swing wildly with the data feed (continuous-contract stitching differs between vendors). So the apparent "winners" in the tables above (Cocoa, NQ, Gasoline, etc.) are largely feed artifacts, not a real edge — they don't reproduce. The one thing both engines agree on is that there is no dependable, repeatable edge in the 4H session method on futures. Silver is the only contract both engines flag profitable, but even there the magnitude differs a lot (C 2.3 vs A 1.25) — interesting, not conclusive.

Why they differ: spot/daily strategies cross-checked within 15% because spot data is consistent across vendors; continuous futures contracts have no clean second feed (each vendor stitches rollovers differently), so this 2-engine test is a robustness check, not an exact match. Low agreement here is itself the signal.

⚠️ Caveats

  • Each contract on its own session candle. 4H bars open at different hours per exchange — CME 3am/7am, CBOT grains 5am/9am, and each ICE soft on its own (coffee 5/9, sugar 4/8, cocoa 6/10). Found empirically so every contract is genuinely tested.
  • Two candles/day = many trades, low win rates — normal for a 2R/3R target.
  • Continuous (1!) contracts, single feed (TradingView) — no clean second futures source for cross-check.
  • Short windows are noisy — weight the 5yr/2yr columns over 6mo.
  • Diagnostic only.

Method: 4H EMA-21 retest + A++ wick (30%), no FVG, pending-stop entry at the wick (1-bar expiry), opposite-wick stop, 3%/trade whole-contract sizing on $100k, 2R & 3R, ~0.01% commission + 1 tick. Session gate: the 4H bar containing the 6:30am PST NY open + the next, per contract. Engine C = TradingView 4H. Windows end ~Jun 2026.
