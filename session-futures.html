TFW 4H Session Strategy — Futures 💜

Amanda's request · A+ entries off the 4H EMA-21, NO FVG · only the 3am & 7am PST candles (grains: 5am & 9am) · both directions · $100k · 3% risk · whole-contract sizing · targets 2R and 3R · cross-checked in TradingView

⚠ Practice test on past data — not real-money results and not a promise of future profit. Diagnostic only.

📖 What was tested

The TFW A+ method on the 4-hour chart: price retests the 4H 21-EMA and rejects with a dominant wick, entered on a pending stop at the wick (filled on the next bar — no look-ahead), stop at the opposite wick, fixed target. The twist Amanda asked for: only take the setup on the 4H candle that opens at 3am or 7am Pacific (the two candles spanning the run-in to, and the open of, the New York session) — and for grains, the 5am & 9am candles. Run on 32 exchange futures at two targets, 2R and 3R.

No look-ahead: the setup is judged only when the session candle closes, and entry is a stop order that can only fill on a later bar — the exact trap that gets session strategies wrong, avoided here.

⚠️ Honest caveats

  • † Grain & soft dollars may be unreliable. Cents-quoted CBOT/ICE continuous contracts can mis-report point value in TradingView, so a few $ columns show "n/a †". Profit factor, trades and win% are still valid.
  • Two session candles per day (3am + 7am PST; grains 5am + 9am) — far more trades than a single-candle version, so win rates sit lower and the edge is thinner per trade.
  • Continuous (1!) contracts — TradingView's stitched front-month series; rollover gaps can occasionally create a signal a single contract wouldn't have.
  • Single-feed — TradingView only; not cross-checked against a second futures data source (continuous futures have no clean second feed).
  • Diagnostic only — for understanding the idea, not a signal to trade.

Method: 4H EMA-21 retest + A++ wick (30%), no FVG, pending-stop entry at the wick (1-bar expiry), opposite-wick stop, risk-based whole-contract sizing 3%/trade on $100k, 2R & 3R targets, ~$0.01% commission + 1 tick. Session gate: hour(open) ∈ {3,7} PST ({5,9} for grains), DST-correct. Engine C = TradingView, 4H, max available history.
